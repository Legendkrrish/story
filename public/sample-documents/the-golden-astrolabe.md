# The Golden Astrolabe

*By Marcus Sterling*

A tale of ancient navigation, starlight, and forgotten oceans.

## Chapter 1: The Harbor of Alexandria

The Mediterranean was calm as liquid obsidian under the new moon. In the basement of the observatory, Master Kael dusted the brass limb of the celestial sphere.

> "A sailor without an astrolabe is merely a leaf on an indifferent sea."

The engravings on the plate revealed thirty-two unknown star systems, aligned not with the skies of today, but with the heavens of five millennia ago.

## Chapter 2: The Voyage Beyond the Pillars

At dawn, the three-masted caravel slipped past the harbor towers. No compass was mounted upon the binnacle; only the glowing golden ring turned in its gymbals.

The crew was silent, watchful. For beyond the Pillars of Hercules lay the western abyss, where maps ended and legends began.
